This is a collection of problems and exercises for the course *CAS CS
320: Concepts of Programming Languages* at Boston University.  They've
been written by [[https://nmmull.github.io][me]] over the last several years of running the course,
with help from [[https://ankushdas.github.io][Ankush Das]].  This page is along the lines of a course
specific

+ [[https://ocaml.org/exercises][OCaml Exercises]]
+ Exericses from [[https://cs3110.github.io/textbook/cover.html][OCaml Programming: Correct + Efficient + Beautiful]]
+ [[https://projecteuler.net][Project Euler]]

all of which are also valuable resources for this course.
