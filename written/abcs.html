ABCs

Consider the following BNF grammar. 

<s> ::= <a> | <b> | <c>
<a> ::= A <b> | A A <b>
<b> ::= B <b> | A B <b> | A B <c>
<c> ::= C | C <c> B
  1. Give two different parse trees of the sentence AABABC.
  2. Find every derivable sentence with 5 (terminal) symbols. Circle (or otherwise mark) any sentences which can be derived in multiple ways.
