Consider the following BNF grammar rule (this is actual OCaml
syntax).

#+begin_src bnf
  <expr> ::= <expr> . [ <expr> ]
#+end_src

Write down an English sentence which expresses the same thing as the
above grammar rule.  In particular, distinguish in your sentence
between the terminal and non-terminal symbols that appear in the rule.
