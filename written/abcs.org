Consider the following BNF grammar.

#+begin_src bnf
  <s> ::= <a> | <b> | <c>
  <a> ::= A <b> | A A <b>
  <b> ::= B <b> | A B <b> | A B <c>
  <c> ::= C | C <c> B
#+end_src

1. Give two different parse trees of the sentence ~AABABC~.
2. Find every derivable sentence with 5 (terminal) symbols.  Circle
   (or otherwise mark) any sentences which can be derived in multiple
   ways.
