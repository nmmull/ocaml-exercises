Consider the following OCaml-like grammar (with start symbol ~<p>~).

#+begin_src bnf
  <p>  ::= <s> <p> | <s>
  <s>  ::= let <v> = <e>
  <e>  ::= let <v> = <e> in <e> | fun <v> -> <e> | <e2>
  <e2> ::= <e2> <e3> | <e3>
  <e3> ::= x | y | z | 0 | 1 | 2 | ( <e> )
  <v>  ::= x | y | z
#+end_src

Demonstrate that the sentence
#+begin_example
  let x = let y = 0 in y
  let z = (fun x -> x) 1
#+end_example
is recognized by the above grammar by providing a parse tree for it.
