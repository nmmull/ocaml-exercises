#+begin_src bnf
  <s> ::= A <s> B | <a>
  <a> ::= A <a> | A <b>
  <b> ::= B B <b> | B B
#+end_src

1. Demonstrate that the sentence ~A A A B B B~ is recognized by the
   above grammar by providing a parse tree for it.
2. Demonstrate that the above grammar is ambiguous by determining
   the *shortest* sentence which has multiple parse trees.  Include
   both parse trees in your solution.
