Top-Level Expressions

Suppose we wanted to allow expressions at the top-level in OCaml. Then we could write 

let x = "foo"
let y = "bar"
print_endline x
print_endline y

This would require something like the following modification to the grammar from the previous problem (only two production rules for <p> have been added). 

<p>  ::= <s> <p> | <s> | <e> <p> | <e>
<s>  ::= let <v> = <e>
<e>  ::= let <v> = <e> in <e> | fun <v> -> <e> | <e2>
<e2> ::= <e2> <e3> | <e3>
<e3> ::= x | y | z | 0 | 1 | 2 | ( <e> )
<v>  ::= x | y | z

Demonstrate that the above grammar is ambiguous by determining a sentence which has multiple parse trees. Include both parse trees in your solution.
