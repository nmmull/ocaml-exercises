Suppose we wanted to allow expressions at the top-level in OCaml.
Then we could write

#+begin_example
  let x = "foo"
  let y = "bar"
  print_endline x
  print_endline y
#+end_example

This would require something like the following modification to
[[file:an-ocaml-like-grammar.org][the grammar from the previous problem]]
(only two production rules for ~<p>~ have been added).

#+begin_src bnf
  <p>  ::= <s> <p> | <s> | <e> <p> | <e>
  <s>  ::= let <v> = <e>
  <e>  ::= let <v> = <e> in <e> | fun <v> -> <e> | <e2>
  <e2> ::= <e2> <e3> | <e3>
  <e3> ::= x | y | z | 0 | 1 | 2 | ( <e> )
  <v>  ::= x | y | z
#+end_src

Demonstrate that the above grammar is ambiguous by determining a
sentence which has multiple parse trees.  Include both parse trees
in your solution.
