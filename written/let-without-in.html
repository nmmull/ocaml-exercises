Let Without In

Suppose we wanted to eliminate the in keyword for local let-expressions in OCaml. Then we could write 

let sum_of_squares x y =
  let x_squared = x * x
  let y_squared = y * y
  x_squared + y_squared

This would require something like the following modification to the grammar from the previous problem (only the first production rule for <e> has been modified). 

<p>  ::= <s> <p> | <s>
<s>  ::= let <v> = <e>
<e>  ::= let <v> = <e> <e> | fun <v> -> <e> | <e2>
<e2> ::= <e2> <e3> | <e3>
<e3> ::= x | y | z | 0 | 1 | 2 | ( <e> )
<v>  ::= x | y | z

Demonstrate that the above grammar is ambiguous by determining a sentence which has multiple parse trees. Include both parse trees in your solution.
