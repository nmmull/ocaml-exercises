A Simple Ambiguous Grammar

Consider the following BNF grammar. 

<s> ::= A <s> B | <a>
<a> ::= A <a> | A <b>
<b> ::= B B <b> | B B
  1. Find the shortest sentence which has multiple parse trees. Also provide two parse trees for your sentence.
  2. Write down a right linear regular grammar for the set of sentences recognized by the above grammar which have at least 2 A symbols and at least 2 B symbols.
  3. Write down a regular expression for the set of sentences recognized by the above grammar.
